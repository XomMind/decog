WHAT IT MEANS IN A RUN

			
    
				
  • A timer starts when you arrive. It rolls a random number of turns from the depth's range (300–1000 at -7..-5, 300–850 at -4..-3, 300–700 at -2..-1), plus 75/150 with RIF Zone Cloak, plus 75 for every Garrison Access you have disabled on this map. Nothing is sent at -8 or deeper.
    • 
				
  • Stepping into a new zone pulls it closer. The map is cut into squares (20×20 in Factory, Research, Access; 15×15 in Storage, Armory, Hub_04(d)...). The first time you stand in a square since the last roll, the timer drops by 15–25 turns. Seeing an area does not count: only where you stand does.
    • 
				
  • When it comes due it re-rolls at once. A squad goes out unless 0b10 sent a timed squad (extermination, intercept, search patrol, assault) in the last 25 turns, or 10 extermination squads are already on the map. Then the cycle is lost, not delayed.
    • 
				
  • They come from an exit you can't see. Squads enter at a random 0b10 exit out of your view, or at a working Garrison Access even if you are looking at it. Cave exits are never used.
    • 
				
  • They search a box around where you stood. ±15 cells around your position at dispatch. After 150 turns the box grows to ±30, 200 turns later to ±45. Every 100 turns on the map the box snaps to your current position, even if they never saw you. While the leader knows where you are, every 10 turns the box re-centres at ±15 and the 150-turn clock restarts.
    • 
				
  • They don't time out. A squad can carry an expiry turn at which the Overmind breaks it up; extermination squads are created with -1, so theirs never comes. They pile up until the timer stops adding more at 10.
    • 
				
  • "Zone 7F" is noise. The ID in the alert is a random digit + letter that doesn't repeat the last ten; it says nothing about where they are going.
    • 
				
  • Q-Series replace Programmers only at -3..-1 (20%, 30%, 50%), and only once 0b10 has analysed your build. They then get weapons picked against it.
    •
